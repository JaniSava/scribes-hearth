The rain fell against the high stained-glass windows of the Abbey like spilled amber beads from a shattered rosary. Alyssa adjusted her wire-frame spectacles, the intricate brass escapement nestled deep in the heel of her palm humming with faint, rhythmic tremors. Through the nave, the shadows smelled of cold stone and forgotten incense.

Below the high altar, where monks had once knelt in penitence, a labyrinth of clockwork rotors turned in perpetual solemnity. Each brass tooth was inscribed with a tiny glyph, turning the immense weight of the cathedral into an altar for Aether-Cores. She paused her quill, listening intently to the cathedral rafters above.

"You tread loudly for a heretic," came a dry whisper from behind the choir stalls. Alyssa did not turn. She knew the dry rattle of that voice, like dry scrolls unfurled in winter. Corvus stepped into the pale light, his leather mantle soaked dark at the shoulders.

He reached beneath his cloak, lifting a small, silvered key that caught the trembling flame of her tallow candle. "They have already sealed the crypt gates. If you wish to reach the Foundry before dawn, you will need more than your father's pocket watch."
